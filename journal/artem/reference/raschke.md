# Linda Raschke: reference notes

Source: her X account, @LindaRaschke. (@LindaRaschke2_ copies her bio and looks like an impersonator.) The rules below come from her posts. She has never published the rules of her scan.

## Two meanings of "TS"

1. **TS scan:** her proprietary opening scan, very likely run in TradeStation, which she says she built about 25 years ago. It posts buy/sell signals in the first 10 to 15 minutes, described as an "impulse up off OP" (OP = the open) and "dogpile footprints."
2. **TS+1:** Turtle Soup Plus One, a failed-breakout reversal setup from *Street Smarts*. It has nothing to do with the scan.

## Her early trend-day markers

- An opening gap.
- No trade below the low of the first 5-minute bar (for longs).
- Three consecutive 5-minute bars in the trend direction in the first 30 minutes: "Three green bars UP off OP in SP in first 30 minutes often leads to trend day UP."
- "Creeper mode": a slow, steady grind.
- An NR7 day alone does not mean a trend day. Light early volume points to chop.

## Day 2: the 15-minute 20 EMA

- The chart: 15-minute, pit session (regular hours only), 20 EMA.
- If a trend day never pulled back to that EMA, it tends to reach it the next day. She calls this near-certain (one miss in 35 years), but the touch can come at any time, even 15:30.
- The touch is a *with-trend* entry, a "grail BUY" after an up trend day, not a reversal. Holy Grail setup: ADX > 30, pullback to the 20 EMA, entry on a break of the pullback bar's high, target a retest of the swing high.
- The day after a trend day: mornings are fade and scalp trades, the afternoon is more trendy. Watch for momentum divergence on the 15-minute chart.
- No bounce statistics are published. Treat it as her stated method, not a tested edge, especially for low-float gappers.
